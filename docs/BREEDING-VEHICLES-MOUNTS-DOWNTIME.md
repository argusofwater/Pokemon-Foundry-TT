# Breeding, Eggs, Vehicles, Mounts, and Downtime

## Breeding and Eggs

Breeding is handled during safe downtime. Ordinary compatible pairings do not require a roll when health, care, food, shelter, and facilities are adequate.

Compatibility may come from Egg Group, species, regional biology, Ditto-like traits, facilities, technology, magic, or story permission. Breeding always requires player and GM consent.

An Egg inherits:

- Species from the designated lineage
- One eligible inherited move
- One inherited Species Talent option
- Nature influence
- One cosmetic or personality trait
- An optional rare trait when a special story or facility permits it

Raw stats are never inherited. The offspring uses normal species growth, Nature, and Training Path rules.

Inherited moves must be known by a parent, anatomically appropriate, and compatible with the offspring. They enter the archived move library. Signature and Legendary moves require explicit story permission.

An inherited Species Talent becomes eligible at the first appropriate milestone rather than activating immediately.

Nature may be rolled, selected from a thematic shortlist, partially guided by facilities or items, or fully chosen by an exceptional breeder feature.

Egg upbringing may grant one modest visible tag such as Hardy, Curious, Calm, Energetic, Clever, Affectionate, Independent, or Unusual. These tags are non-stackable and provide only small situational benefits.

## Egg stages

Egg development uses three stages:

- Egg
- Hatching
- Juvenile

Eggs cannot battle. They track Incubation Progress, health, environment, caregiver, and known species information.

Hatching is a scene or milestone that may reveal Nature, inherited traits, Bond opportunities, or a special complication.

Juvenile Pokémon may Bond, learn, assist safely in social or expedition scenes, and develop personality, but are not battle-ready until the story and GM establish readiness.

## Incubation Progress

Suggested Incubation targets:

- Common species: 3
- Uncommon species: 5
- Rare species: 8
- Exceptional species: 12
- Legendary or artificial Egg: story-gated

Progress comes from safe rest, correct temperature, proper care, nursery equipment, suitable abilities, incubators, and breeder or Medic Talents.

Poor care slows progress or creates a complication. It does not casually destroy an Egg.

The primary caregiver affects starting Bond:

- Neglected or absent care: Wary
- Normal care: Trusting
- Dedicated care: Trusting plus one Bond milestone
- Exceptional story care: Bonded with GM approval

Breeding support belongs in Specialties and Talents rather than a separate class tree.

## Vehicles

Vehicle categories include bicycles, motorcycles, ground vehicles, watercraft, aircraft, rail vehicles, and specialized expedition vehicles.

Every vehicle lists:

- Size
- Speed
- Crew
- Passengers
- Cargo
- Handling
- Integrity
- Armor
- Fuel or Power
- Terrain tags
- Equipment slots

Vehicle size uses Personal, Small, Medium, Large, and Massive. Travel speed uses Slow, Standard, Fast, Very Fast, and Extreme.

Handling modifiers are:

- Clumsy: -4
- Heavy: -2
- Standard: +0
- Responsive: +2
- Agile: +4

At half Integrity, a vehicle becomes Damaged. At 0 Integrity, it becomes Disabled. Further damage may require a Crash Check.

Vehicle conditions include Damaged, Disabled, Burning, Breached, Stalled, Unstable, Jammed, and Out of Power.

Heavy vehicle weapons use a Scale tag. Scale improves attacks against structures, vehicles, and Massive targets. Against ordinary creatures, Scale should usually create area, forced movement, or environmental pressure instead of unrestricted extra damage dice.

## Vehicle chases

Chases use a Lead track:

- Pursuer +3
- Pursuer +2
- Pursuer +1
- Even
- Quarry +1
- Quarry +2
- Quarry +3

At Quarry +3, the quarry escapes. At Pursuer +3, the pursuer catches or corners the quarry.

Each chase round:

1. Both sides choose a maneuver.
2. Drivers make checks.
3. Lead shifts.
4. Hazards and other actions resolve.

Maneuvers include Accelerate, Take Shortcut, Ram, Block, Hide, Stunt, Brace, Attack, and Deploy Pokémon.

Vehicle crew roles are Driver, Navigator, Gunner, Engineer, and Commander. Small vehicles may combine roles.

A suitable Pokémon may provide one Power Unit per expedition interval, but doing so counts as strenuous activity.

## Mounts

A Pokémon may serve as a mount when its size and anatomy are suitable, it has the Mount capability, it accepts the rider, and required gear or training is available.

Mounts may provide faster travel, terrain access, reduced Fatigue, greater carrying capacity, combat mobility, or evacuation.

Mounted combat uses linked movement:

- The mount supplies movement.
- The rider keeps their actions.
- A combat-trained mount may still use its own Main Action.
- Forced movement affects both.
- Dismounting normally costs a Move Action.
- If the mount falls Prone, the rider makes a Reflex or Acrobatics check.

Mounted charges may grant +1 reach, +2 damage, or forced movement, but only one charge benefit applies per round.

Flying mounts require takeoff and landing space, suffer load limits, and may be affected by severe Weather or confined environments.

## Downtime

One week of meaningful downtime grants:

- Two Downtime Actions per trainer
- One shared facility action for the group when a facility is available

Short breaks may grant one action. Longer breaks may grant more.

Downtime activities include:

- Train Pokémon
- Research
- Craft
- Earn Income
- Build Reputation
- Recover
- Breed and Raise
- Find Opportunity
- Maintain Vehicle
- Personal Project

Long projects use Progress:

- Small: 3
- Meaningful: 5
- Major: 8
- Exceptional: 12+

Each Downtime Action normally allows one check. Success gains 1 Progress. Strong success gains 2. Failure causes cost, delay, or complication but does not normally erase prior Progress.

## Facilities and bases

Facilities include Pokémon Centers, nurseries, laboratories, workshops, dojos, Contest Halls, Ranger Stations, vehicle garages, botanical gardens, and libraries.

Facilities may grant Favored, reduce cost, allow special recipes, increase Progress, remove risk, or unlock activities.

A group base may track Location, Size, Facilities, Staff, Storage, Reputation, Upkeep, and Projects.

Base upkeep uses one broad campaign cost. Staff handle routine operations while players choose upgrades and major projects.

## Foundry support

Foundry should track:

- Breeding compatibility
- Parent records
- Inherited move and Talent eligibility
- Incubation Progress
- Egg, Hatching, and Juvenile stages
- Caregiver and Bond
- Vehicle profiles and Integrity
- Fuel or Power
- Crew roles
- Vehicle conditions
- Chase Lead
- Mount linkage
- Downtime Actions
- Project Progress
- Facilities
- Base improvements

Compendiums should contain Egg traits, breeder Talents, vehicles, vehicle equipment, chase hazards, facilities, downtime activities, and project templates.

## Locked decisions

- Breeding through safe downtime rather than routine checks
- One inherited move and one inherited Species Talent option
- No inherited raw stats or hidden IV system
- Incubation Progress
- Egg, Hatching, and Juvenile stages
- Caregiver-based starting Bond
- Breeder Talents instead of a separate breeding class
- Simple vehicle profiles using Integrity and a short condition list
- Lead-based chase rules
- Vehicle crew roles
- Mount capability and linked mounted movement
- Two Downtime Actions per week
- Progress-based long projects
- Facilities and optional group-base support
- Full Foundry tracking for Eggs, vehicles, mounts, and downtime
